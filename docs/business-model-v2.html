LeadScale Systems — Business Model Summary

AI-native back office · Australian beauty vertical · Two-person partnership, Brisbane
Status: pre-validation

We take one recurring, repetitive back-office job off small appointment-based businesses and deliver it as completed work — not software. Sold at low ticket to many clients, priced against a labour line rather than a software subscription.

Customer

  • Australian owner-operated beauty businesses
  • Hair, beauty, massage, tanning, nails
  • 1–10 staff, single site
  • Existing booking software, no marketing function
  • Owner does admin after close

Problem — hypothesis only

Client bases decay silently. Nobody works the lapsed list, cancellations aren't chased, and the loss is invisible because it produces no event.

Unverified. Sourced from vendor benchmark content, not from owners. Discovery may replace it — staffing and front-of-house are the leading rivals.

Offer — provisional

Rolling reactivation and rebooking, run as a service:

  • Overdue-client triggers, continuous
  • Rebooking prompts at ~80% of interval
  • Same-day cancellation recovery
  • No-show follow-up
  • Monthly results report

Why we can win

  • Warm network in the vertical — solves distribution, the constraint that kills most bootstrapped services
  • Developer in-house — we build, not resell
  • No API dependency — export-based, so it works on Fresha (which has no public API at all)
  • Spam Act literate — most operators in this space are not

Distribution

  • Walk-ins — primary. Tue/Wed mornings. No legal friction, best response rate, richest data.
  • Warm network — first 5 conversations
  • Community group archives, then admin-approved posts
  • Email to published business addresses
  • SMS — thin supplement only. Prospect data confirms listed numbers are almost entirely landlines.
  • Referral engineered from client one

The actual asset

Not the retainer. Two things compound:

  • Cross-client behavioural data no single salon can generate
  • A monthly report sitting in 50 owners' inboxes — the launchpad for product two

Services now, software later. Build the data schema from client one.

Before the first dollar

  • PI insurance confirmed in writing to cover AI-delivered work — insurers added absolute AI exclusions in 2026
  • Client agreement: liability capped, scope defined, consent warranted
  • Spam Act architecture: 18–24 month lookback cap, sender identification, functional unsubscribe, permanent global suppression list

Next 90 days

  • ✓ Prospect list built — Brisbane discovery tool shipped; ~600 businesses with names and suburbs, walk-in list ready.
  • Now — archive mining, then 12–15 discovery conversations. Confirm or replace the hypothesis. Insurance and agreement in parallel.
  • Weeks 3–6 — three founding clients, near-free, delivered entirely by hand. Time every task. Produce real numbers and a written SOP.
  • Weeks 7–12 — ten paying clients at full price. No discounts. Delivery under 90 min/client/month. Automate only what's been proven manually.

Kill criteria

  • <50% raise the problem unprompted
  • Owners can't quantify the loss
  • Their platform already solves it well
  • Can't deliver under 1.5 hr/client/month
  • Nobody has ever paid to fix it
